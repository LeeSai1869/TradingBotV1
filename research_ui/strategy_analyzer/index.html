2. Configure your strategy

Branches for the same side are alternatives. A regular SELL exits at the next open. Take profit places a target while its conditions hold. A trailing SELL uses its conditions plus its profit threshold to arm a standing trail; once armed it stays armed. Conditions use closed candles: 0=current, 1=previous, 2=two ago. Other SELL branches can exit earlier.
