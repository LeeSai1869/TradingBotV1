ENAUSDT 15m — offline pivot-rule trial

Research comparison on 6,000 exported candles. The proposed rule requires a reversal of at least 25% of the last two confirmed pivots’ range AND two consecutive reverse closes. No app, runner or database has been changed.

RuleConfirmationsAdjacent-candle confirmationsMedian origin-to-confirmation candle lag

The first exported candle supplies the known confirmed pair and active candidate. Close counts start at zero. A new extreme resets the count. Equal/interrupted closes reset it. All confirmation decisions use closed candles only. The opposite candidate retains the extreme since the previous pivot’s origin, excluding that origin candle because intrabar high/low ordering is unknown. This standalone trial does not run BOS, classifications or any trading strategy.
